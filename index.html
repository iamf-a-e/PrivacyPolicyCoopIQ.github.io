The short version

  • We collect what we need to answer your poultry and livestock questions: your WhatsApp number, your messages and voice notes, and any photos you send. If you use the app, we also collect your email address.
  • Your messages, voice notes and photos are processed by AI services (including Google Gemini) to produce answers and diagnoses.
  • We keep your conversation history so the assistant remembers what you talked about. It does not expire on its own. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us (see Contact us).

1. Who we are

CoopIQ is a poultry and livestock health service. We offer an AI assistant on WhatsApp, a mobile app with photo-based diagnosis, and connections to agrovet tele-consultations and farm visits. In this policy, "CoopIQ", "we", "us" and "our" mean the business that runs these services.

This policy applies to the CoopIQ WhatsApp assistant, the CoopIQ mobile app, and the consultations and farm visits we arrange. It explains what we do with your personal information. If you disagree with any part of it, please do not use the service.

2. Information we collect

What we collect depends on how you use CoopIQ.

When you message us on WhatsApp

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, and the profile name WhatsApp shares with us when you message.
  • Your messages: the text you type, the buttons you tap, and the voice notes you record. This may include the language you use and details about your farm, flock or animals.
  • Our replies to you.
  • Technical details such as message IDs and time stamps, which we use to deliver messages and avoid sending duplicate replies.

When you use the mobile app

  • Account details: your email address and a password. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and stored in a protected form, so we cannot read it.
  • Photos and questions you submit for diagnosis, and the results you receive.
  • Settings you choose, such as your language.

When you ask for a consultation or farm visit

  • Your name, phone number and the location of your farm.
  • A description of the problem, and any photos or records you share.

Collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as IP address, device type and app version, and server logs. Our hosting and authentication providers process this to keep the service running and secure.
  • Our server logs may include the content of incoming messages and the sender's phone number for a limited period while we operate and troubleshoot the service.

Please avoid sending information that is not needed to answer your question, such as national ID numbers, bank details or passwords.

3. How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and give poultry and livestock guidance in the language you use.
  • To understand voice notes by converting them to text.
  • To analyse photos and suggest possible diagnoses.
  • To remember earlier messages in your conversation, so you do not have to repeat yourself.
  • To create and secure your account, and to send account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password reset.
  • To arrange tele-consultations and farm visits when you ask for them.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems, and prevent abuse.
  • To improve the accuracy and quality of CoopIQ.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We rely on your consent and on our need to provide the service you asked for. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us, although we may then be unable to provide some or all of the service.

We use trusted companies to run CoopIQ. They process your information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service. Their own privacy policies also apply.

Important to know about AI. AI answers can be wrong. CoopIQ gives general guidance and is not a substitute for a qualified veterinarian, especially for dosages, medicines and serious disease outbreaks. We do not control how Google or the other providers handle data under their own terms, so please read their policies if you want details.

We do not keep the audio file of your voice notes. We send it to be transcribed, and the transcript is saved as part of your conversation history.

5. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with the service providers listed above, to run CoopIQ;
  • with an agrovet, veterinarian or field officer, when you ask for a consultation or farm visit and they need your details to help you;
  • when the law requires it, for example in response to a valid request from a court or authority;
  • to protect the safety, rights or property of our users, the public or CoopIQ; and
  • if CoopIQ is merged with, or taken over by, another business, in which case your information may transfer to the new owner and this policy will continue to apply until you are told otherwise.

If we publish research or reports, we will use information that has been combined and anonymised so that it does not identify you.

6. How long we keep information

  • Conversation history: kept so the assistant can remember earlier messages. We do not delete it automatically. We delete it when you ask us to.
  • Account and app data: kept while your account is active, and deleted when you ask us to close it.
  • Consultation and visit records: kept for as long as needed to follow up with you and to meet any legal or record-keeping duties.
  • Server logs: kept for a limited time by our hosting provider, according to its settings.
  • Backups: deleted information may remain in backups for a short time before it is removed.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your conversation history, your account or other information we hold;
  • stop using your information for a purpose you do not agree with, or withdraw your consent; and
  • explain how your information is used.

To make a request, contact us using the details in Contact us. We may need to confirm that you are the owner of the phone number or account first. We will respond within a reasonable time.

You can also stop using CoopIQ at any time by no longer messaging us or by deleting the app. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the data protection authority in your country.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access controls on our systems and our providers' systems. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you think your account or conversation has been accessed without your permission, please tell us straight away.

9. International transfers

Our service providers operate servers in several countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live, including outside Zimbabwe and Zambia. When this happens, we rely on providers that apply safeguards for personal information, and we take steps so that your information is treated in line with this policy and applicable law.

10. Children

CoopIQ is meant for farmers and other adults. It is not aimed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you through WhatsApp or the app. Using CoopIQ after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us

For questions about this policy, or to make a request about your information, contact us:

  • Email: your privacy contact email
  • WhatsApp: message us on the CoopIQ WhatsApp number and say that your message is about privacy.
  • Address: your registered business address

This policy is governed by the laws of country of registration, together with any data protection laws that apply to you where you live.
